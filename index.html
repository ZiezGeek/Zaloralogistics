Who We Are

Company
Overview

Zalora Logistics (Pty) Ltd is a proudly South African, female youth-owned logistics company specialising in reliable road freight transportation of bulk goods across major routes in South Africa.

As a 100% Black female-owned and Level 1 B-BBEE contributor, Zalora Logistics is committed to excellence, empowerment and transformation within the logistics industry — driving meaningful change while consistently delivering professional, on-time services.

We are not just moving goods — we're driving empowerment, progress and opportunity across South Africa.

What We Do

Our Services

Road Freight Transport — Primary Focus

Specialising in the transportation of bulk goods and general cargo across South Africa's key routes — including Durban, Johannesburg, Cape Town and surrounding regions.

Full Truck Load (FTL)

Providing flexible transport options for clients who require either dedicated or shared trucking services, ensuring cost-efficiency and reliability.

Industrial & Manufacturing Logistics

Transport of raw materials and finished goods for manufacturers, supporting a seamless and uninterrupted supply chain flow.

Agricultural Transport

Safe and efficient movement of agricultural commodities, including fertilisers, grains and farming equipment.

Contract Logistics & Long-Term Partnerships

Tailored logistics contracts designed for businesses with ongoing transportation requirements, ensuring consistency, transparency and cost optimisation.
